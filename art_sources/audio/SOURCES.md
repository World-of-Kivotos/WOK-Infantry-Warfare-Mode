# Medical Foley sources

The in-game `.ogg` files are edited derivatives of real recordings. They are
trimmed, mixed, converted to mono/48 kHz, faded and peak-normalised by
`tools/generate_medical_sounds.py`.

## Used recordings

- `syringe_taure_465495.mp3`
  - “syringe.wav” by taure
  - https://freesound.org/people/taure/sounds/465495/
  - Creative Commons 0
  - Used for the close syringe and plunger movement.

- `needle_fabric_wavjunction_456772.mp3`
  - “Sewing Pin Through Fabric.wav” by WavJunction.com
  - https://freesound.org/people/WavJunction.com/sounds/456772/
  - Creative Commons 0
  - Used for the short, dry puncture layer.

- `canvas_zipper_vintage2005_438985.mp3`
  - “Zipper for a tent or large bag” by vintage2005
  - https://freesound.org/people/vintage2005/sounds/438985/
  - Creative Commons 0
  - Used for opening a medical kit.

- `wrapping_cloth_chucklenutsdev_718668.mp3`
  - “Wrapping cloth” by ChuckleNutsDev
  - https://freesound.org/people/ChuckleNutsDev/sounds/718668/
  - Creative Commons 0
  - Recorded specifically to emulate bandage wrapping; used for bandages and
    ongoing medical-kit treatment.

The checked-in MP3 files are Freesound’s public high-quality previews. The
Creative Commons 0 license permits copying, modification and redistribution.
